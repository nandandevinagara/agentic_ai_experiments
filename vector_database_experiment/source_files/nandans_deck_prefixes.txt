What type of prefix is "ab-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "off, away" (e.g. abfahren = to depart)
What type of prefix is "an-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "on, at" (e.g. ankommen = to arrive)
What type of prefix is "auf-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "up, open" (e.g. aufmachen = to open)
What type of prefix is "aus-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "out, off" (e.g. ausgehen = to go out)
What type of prefix is "ein-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "in, into" (e.g. einsteigen = to get in)
What type of prefix is "mit-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "with, along" (e.g. mitkommen = to come along)
What type of prefix is "vor-" and what does it mean?	Separable prefix — means "before, in front of" (e.g. vorstellen = to introduce)
What type of prefix is "be-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — makes a verb transitive (e.g. besuchen = to visit)
What type of prefix is "ent-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — means "away, escaping" (e.g. entfliehen = to escape)
What type of prefix is "er-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — means "completing, achieving" (e.g. erreichen = to reach)
What type of prefix is "ge-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — often a perfect tense marker in other contexts (e.g. gewinnen = to win)
What type of prefix is "ver-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — means "bad or wrong, often changes meaning" (e.g. verstehen = to understand)
What type of prefix is "zer-" and what does it mean?	Inseparable prefix — means "destroying or breaking" (e.g. zerbrechen = to break apart)
What type of prefix is "durch-" and what does it mean?	Ambivalent prefix — means "through" (can be separable or inseparable)
What type of prefix is "über-" and what does it mean?	Ambivalent prefix — means "over, across" (can be separable or inseparable)
What type of prefix is "um-" and what does it mean?	Ambivalent prefix — means "around, changing" (can be separable or inseparable)
What type of prefix is "unter-" and what does it mean?	Ambivalent prefix — means "under" (can be separable or inseparable)
Name all separable prefixes in German.	ab-, an-, auf-, aus-, ein-, mit-, vor-
Name all inseparable prefixes in German.	be-, ent-, er-, ge-, ver-, zer-
Name all ambivalent prefixes in German.	durch-, über-, um-, unter-
What is the difference between separable and inseparable prefixes?	Separable prefixes split off to the end of the sentence in main clauses. Inseparable prefixes always stay attached to the verb.
What makes ambivalent prefixes special?	They can be either separable or inseparable depending on the verb and meaning.
